# Mel-Co-Ed redesign review

The development branch continues from commit 5121cc7. Cloudflare Pages builds branch previews with the repository’s existing Astro build. Production releases require the owner’s separate decision.

## Buyers and content

The homepage pairs three labeled design concepts with genuine Open Line floral stampings in the product collection. The red-and-brass badge is the original company mark. Shop and material photography comes from the existing approved collection. Fictional badge and medallion concepts carry visible captions. The public catalog PDFs are the existing Open Line catalogs. The revised At a Glance sheet reflects project-specific minimum quantities and is available at the previous download URL.

Product, industry and capability pages lead buyers to relevant guides and the quote form. The form asks for initial quantity and planned reorders. When configured, it uses the existing Web3Forms integration. Otherwise it prepares an email and a copyable summary for the sales inbox. The buyer sends the email and attaches files in their own mail app.

## Review notes

Check the homepage, a product page, a buyer guide, and Request a Quote at both phone and desktop widths. The staging test can validate required fields and email-draft preparation without delivering an inquiry. Receipt of a real inquiry in the sales inbox requires a separate delivery test. The original catalog introductions use broad material-availability wording. Production material and finish availability are confirmed at quote.

The build generates static HTML, optimized images, Pagefind search and a sitemap. Production canonical URLs are intentional. Cloudflare's preview noindex header keeps the review version out of search. Existing redirects continue to apply.
